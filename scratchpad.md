# Market Eye AI — Pitch Deck

**Audience:** Firecrawl judges, Datadog judges, general hackathon judges
**Duration:** ~5 min (10 slides)
**Vibe:** Clean modern SaaS, light/crisp. Orange accent (#ff5a1f — also nods to Firecrawl).

## Title sequence (short topic noun-phrases, textbook style)
1. Market Eye AI — cover
2. The Problem
3. The Idea
4. How It Works
5. Powered by Firecrawl
6. Product Discovery
7. Competitor Monitor   (Datadog = observability angle)
8. Brand & Store Builder
9. Why It Wins
10. The Vision — closing

Flow check: Problem → Idea → How → the engine (Firecrawl) → 3 module deep-dives → why it wins → vision. Reads as a clean chapter sequence.

## Design system
- Fonts: Manrope (display + body, weight contrast), JetBrains Mono (endpoint chips, data labels, placeholder captions)
- Colors:
  - --bg warm white #fbfbf9
  - --ink #17171a
  - --muted #6b6b70
  - --line #e7e6e1
  - --accent #ff5a1f
  - --accent-soft #fff1ea
  - dark slide: #111114 bg, #f4f3f0 text
- Layout language: lots of whitespace, thin rules, mono kicker labels (e.g. "01 — PROBLEM"), orange used sparingly for emphasis + the eye dot.
- Section parallelism: every slide has a top kicker (NN — LABEL) + thin rule. Module slides (6,7,8) share the same split layout w/ striped placeholder mock.
- Screenshots: striped placeholders w/ mono labels (user chose "decide for me").

## Slide plan (keep text light!)
1. Cover: wordmark + eye dot, tagline, mono sublabel
2. Problem: big statement + 3 mono pain items (blind to trends / blind to competitors / weeks to build)
3. Idea: "Idea in. Live store out." + one support line + mini flow teaser
4. How It Works: 5-step horizontal stepper (Scan → Analyze → Brand → Launch → Grow)
5. Powered by Firecrawl: 4 endpoint chips (search/scrape/crawl/extract) each w/ one-line role
6. Product Discovery: split — placeholder mock + 3 capability lines
7. Competitor Monitor: split — placeholder dashboard + observability framing (alerts)
8. Brand & Store Builder: split — brand outputs → Shopify store live
9. Why It Wins: 3 reasons (end-to-end / real-time data moat / democratizes e-comm) OR big number
10. Vision: closing statement + wordmark + CTA
